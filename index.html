    My journey

    My path into DevOps.

    From IT support into DevOps, building on the skills and lessons from each stage.

    01Foundation

    IT Support

    Working directly with systems, users and faults built a practical troubleshooting mindset before I moved into infrastructure engineering.

    WindowsActive DirectoryMicrosoft 365SQL
    02Foundation

    Linux & Networking

    Learning operating systems, permissions, networking and the command line gave me the foundations needed to understand cloud infrastructure properly.

    LinuxBashTCP/IPDNS
    03Infrastructure

    Cloud

    I moved from individual systems to thinking about networks, compute, identity and services as connected infrastructure.

    AWSVPCEC2IAM
    04Containers

    Docker

    Containerisation changed how I thought about packaging applications, dependencies and repeatable environments.

    DockerComposeNGINXRedis
    05Infrastructure as Code

    Terraform

    Infrastructure became something I could define, review and reproduce rather than build manually.

    TerraformStateProvidersModules

    I began working with orchestration concepts through local clusters and Amazon EKS, focusing on how container workloads are deployed and operated.

    KuberneteskindEKSkubectl
