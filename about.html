About

From IT support into DevOps.

I started in IT support, where I learned to diagnose issues across users, systems and services. That gave me a practical troubleshooting foundation. DevOps became the next step: moving from resolving individual incidents to understanding how infrastructure is built, changed and automated.

>_ route --open
My background

Support gave me the foundation. DevOps widened the view.

My earlier work in second-line support meant dealing directly with operational issues across Active Directory, Microsoft 365, VPN and firewall access, server checks, SQL maintenance tasks and remote support tooling. It taught me to gather evidence, understand the environment and work methodically before making a change.

As I developed technically, I became increasingly interested in what sat behind those day-to-day incidents: how environments are provisioned, how applications are deployed, how infrastructure changes are controlled and how repetitive work can be automated. That progression led me towards cloud infrastructure, containers, infrastructure as code and CI/CD.

Today, I work in DevOps engineering while continuing to strengthen those areas through hands-on projects. The goal is not simply to collect tools, but to understand how they fit together to make systems more repeatable, observable and easier to operate.

How I work

What I bring with me.

The support background still influences how I approach engineering work today.

Engineering approach

Understand before changing.

Support work taught me not to jump immediately to a fix. I try to understand the symptoms, dependencies and likely failure points before changing infrastructure or configuration.

That mindset carries naturally into troubleshooting deployments, infrastructure plans and cloud environments.

Communication

Make technical work understandable.

I document what I build and what I learn because being able to explain a system is part of understanding it properly.

That has also shaped how I create technical content and contribute to community and digital projects outside day-to-day engineering.

Current focus

Building depth, not just breadth.

I’m continuing to develop around infrastructure as code, cloud platforms, container workloads, delivery pipelines and observability, with projects that show the architecture, implementation, troubleshooting and production considerations behind the work.
