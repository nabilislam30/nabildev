Background

From support into engineering

I started in second-line support, working directly with users, systems and faults across Active Directory, Microsoft 365, Windows Server, SQL and VPNs. That gave me a practical troubleshooting foundation and made me want to move closer to the infrastructure behind the services I was supporting.

I joined CoderCo to build hands-on DevOps experience across Linux, cloud, Terraform, containers, CI/CD and Kubernetes. I now apply those foundations in a DevOps engineering role while continuing to build projects and labs outside work.
