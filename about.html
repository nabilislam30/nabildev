Background

My background

I didn’t start out as a DevOps Engineer. For three years, I worked in second-line support, helping end users across technologies such as Active Directory, Microsoft 365, Windows Server, SQL and VPNs. Over time, I decided that I wanted to move into a role where I could build and work more closely with infrastructure rather than primarily support it.

I joined CoderCo to help me move into DevOps, gaining hands-on experience through projects and live labs across cloud, infrastructure, containers and CI/CD. It helped prepare me for my first DevOps role, and I’ve continued building on those skills through professional work and personal projects.
