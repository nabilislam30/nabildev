About me

How I got into DevOps

I’m a DevOps Engineer with three years of experience in second-line support. That background still shapes how I troubleshoot problems, understand systems and approach infrastructure work.

>_ route --open
Background

My background

I spent three years in second-line support, working across Active Directory, Microsoft 365, Windows Server, SQL and VPNs. I enjoyed the troubleshooting side of the role, but I wanted to move closer to the infrastructure itself—building systems rather than only supporting them.

That led me to CoderCo, hands-on DevOps projects and eventually my first DevOps role. I now contribute to infrastructure changes, deployments and automation while continuing to develop my AWS, Kubernetes and system design knowledge through personal projects.

My approach

How I approach the work

01

Start with the problem

I start by working out what is failing and why. My support background taught me to investigate before changing things.

02

Automate repeatable work

If a task is repeated or easy to get wrong, I look for a way to make it consistent through code, pipelines or clear documentation.

03

Understand the whole system

I want to understand how networking, identity, compute and applications interact—not just how to use one tool.
